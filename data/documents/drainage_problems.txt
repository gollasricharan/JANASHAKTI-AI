Drainage and Waterlogging Guidelines

Residents should report blocked drains, overflowing
sewage, and waterlogging in residential areas.

Complaints should include the affected location,
duration of the problem, and approximate number
of affected households.

Blocked drainage systems may cause flooding,
unpleasant odors, and sanitation problems.

Residents should avoid contact with contaminated
water and report serious drainage problems
to the appropriate municipal authority.

Authorities should inspect the drainage system
and determine suitable corrective action.
