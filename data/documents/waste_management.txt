Waste Management Guidelines

Residents should report uncollected garbage, overflowing
dustbins, and illegal waste dumping.

Complaints should include the location, date, description
of the problem, and photographs if available.

Residents should contact the local municipal corporation
or responsible waste management department.

Garbage accumulation may create sanitation problems,
attract pests, and cause unpleasant odors.

Municipal authorities should verify complaints and
arrange appropriate waste collection or cleanup.
