Community Complaint Reporting Guidelines

Residents can submit complaints about community
problems such as water shortages, electricity
outages, damaged roads, and waste management.

A complaint should include:

1. Category of the problem
2. State and district
3. Locality or affected area
4. Detailed description
5. Date and duration of the issue
6. Supporting evidence if available

Each submitted complaint should receive
a unique complaint identification number.

Complaint Status:

Submitted - Complaint has been registered.

Under Investigation - Complaint is being reviewed.

Resolved - The responsible authority has addressed
the reported problem.

Verification should be completed before a complaint
is treated as confirmed.

AI-generated recommendations should support
human decision-making rather than replace it.
