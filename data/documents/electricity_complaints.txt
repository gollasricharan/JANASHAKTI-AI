Electricity Complaint Guidelines

Residents experiencing electricity outages should report
the affected locality, date, time, and duration of the outage.

If multiple households are affected, residents should
mention the approximate number of affected households.

Electricity complaints should be submitted to the
appropriate electricity distribution authority.

For damaged electrical poles, exposed wires, or fallen
power lines, residents should maintain a safe distance
and contact the responsible authority immediately.

Residents should keep their complaint reference number
for tracking and follow-up.
