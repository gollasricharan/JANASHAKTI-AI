Streetlight Complaint Guidelines

Residents should report broken streetlights,
flickering lights, and poorly illuminated streets.

Complaints should include the street name,
locality, nearby landmarks, and pole number
if available.

Nonfunctional streetlights may increase safety
risks for pedestrians and road users at night.

Residents should report streetlight problems
through official municipal complaint channels.

The responsible department should inspect
the streetlight and arrange necessary repairs.
