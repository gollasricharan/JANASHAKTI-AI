Public Safety Guidelines

Residents should report unsafe public infrastructure,
open manholes, exposed electrical wires,
and other potential public hazards.

Complaints should include the exact location,
description of the hazard, and supporting
photographs if available.

Residents should avoid approaching dangerous
locations or attempting unsafe repairs.

For immediate threats to life or safety,
residents should contact the appropriate
emergency services.

Authorities should assess reported hazards
and determine suitable safety measures.
